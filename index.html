The two humans behind the fins.

SuperPod was created by daughter-daddy duo Malia Westheimer and Cody Westheimer.

Their shared love of orcas began at home — through stories, questions, and imagination — and eventually led them to experience orcas together in the wild.

Malia is a fifth grader who loves nature, animals, and a good adventure. Cody is a composer whose primary career is writing music for film and television. In fact, you can listen to the soundtrack of SuperPod wherever you stream your music!
